# 🏢 Google Business Profile Setup Guide - SystroCode

## 🎯 **Objective**: Create DA 97 Backlink via Google Business Profile

### **Step-by-Step Implementation**

#### **Phase 1: Profile Creation**
1. **Go to Google Business Profile**: https://business.google.com/
2. **Add Your Business**: Click "Manage now" or "Add your business"
3. **Business Information**:
   - **Business Name**: SystroCode
   - **Category**: Software Development Company / Web Development Services
   - **Location**: Add your business address (if applicable)
   - **Service Areas**: Specify regions you serve
   - **Website**: https://systrocode.com
   - **Phone**: Business contact number

#### **Phase 2: Profile Optimization**

##### **Business Description** (750 characters max):
```
SystroCode delivers cutting-edge software development solutions including web applications, mobile development, AI automation, and digital marketing services. Specializing in Next.js, React, and modern web technologies, we help businesses scale with custom software solutions, SEO optimization, and comprehensive digital transformation strategies.
```

##### **Review Generation Strategy**:
1. **Client Outreach**: Request reviews from satisfied clients
2. **Follow-up System**: Send review requests post-project completion
3. **Response Protocol**: Respond to all reviews professionally
4. **Review Monitoring**: Weekly check for new reviews
